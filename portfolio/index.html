About

  

    

      
I'm Shritij, a Computer Science graduate from KMIT and a Data Science and Programming student at IIT Madras. I recently completed an Applied Scientist internship at Amazon in Bangalore, where I worked on image similarity and image-quality detection, and cleared the bar for an Applied Scientist I role.

      
Before that, I spent nine months at Techolution building speech pipelines, multi-agent LLM systems and RAG backends that served production traffic. Today I work as a part-time research assistant on LLM evaluation, and I have three papers under review on LLM judges and tool-using agents.

      
I've won three national hackathons — including first place at Smart India Hackathon 2024 — and I founded Upsolve Club, an AI-native platform for competitive coders. I like problems that sit between research and real products.

      
Speaks English · Telugu · Hindi · Tamil · Bengali
